# Portal controls

> Written in my own words from general industry knowledge. No employer code, data, schemas or documents.

## Entitlements & roles
- Who can do what, and who decides?
- How are roles granted, changed and removed?
- How is least privilege checked over time?

## Maker-checker
- Which actions need a second person?
- What stops the maker and checker being the same person?
- What happens when the checker rejects?

## Transaction limits
- What kinds of limits exist (per transaction, per day, per user, per account)?
- Who sets and changes limits, and is that change itself controlled?
- What happens when a limit is hit?

## Audit trail
- What is recorded for each action (who, what, when, from where)?
- Can the trail be altered, and by whom?
- How long is it kept, and who reviews it?

## How portal activity reaches core banking
- What path does an instruction take from the portal to core banking?
- Where can it be held, rejected or duplicated along the way?
- How are the two sides reconciled?

## What evidence each control produces
- For each control above, what record proves it ran?
- Where is that evidence stored, and who can retrieve it?
- What would an auditor ask to see?
